URBAN THREAD CO. — RETURN & REFUND POLICY

1. RETURN WINDOW
Customers may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund or exchange. Items returned after 30 days but within 60 days are eligible for store credit only. No returns or refunds will be accepted after 60 days from delivery.

2. ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS
To qualify for a return, items must be:
- Unworn, unwashed, and unused
- In their original packaging with all tags attached
- Accompanied by the original receipt or proof of purchase (order ID is sufficient)

The following items are FINAL SALE and cannot be returned or refunded:
- Items marked "Clearance" or "Final Sale" at time of purchase
- Earrings and other pierced jewelry, for hygiene reasons
- Gift cards

3. REFUND METHOD AND TIMING
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days. Customers should allow an additional 3-5 business days for the refund to reflect on their bank or card statement, depending on their financial institution.

4. DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, OR INCORRECT ITEMS
If a customer receives a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, Urban Thread Co. will cover return shipping costs and issue a full refund or replacement at no additional charge. Customers must report such issues within 7 days of delivery, along with photos of the issue, to qualify for free return shipping.

5. RETURN SHIPPING COSTS
For returns not related to damage, defect, or our error (e.g., customer changed their mind, wrong size ordered), the customer is responsible for return shipping costs. A flat return shipping fee of $5.99 will be deducted from the refund amount unless the customer is a Loyalty Program member, in which case return shipping is free.

6. RESTOCKING FEE
A 10% restocking fee applies to returns of bulky items (bags, backpacks, and footwear) unless the return is due to damage, defect, or fulfillment error on our part.

7. EXCHANGES
Customers may exchange an item for a different size or color of the same product, subject to availability. Exchanges follow the same 30-day eligibility window as refunds. If the desired size/color is out of stock, the customer will be offered a full refund or store credit instead.

8. HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN
Customers should contact customer support with their order ID and reason for return. Once approved, customers will receive a return shipping label (if applicable) and instructions for returning the item. Items should be shipped back within 14 days of return approval.

9. INTERNATIONAL ORDERS
International orders are eligible for the same 30-day return window. Customers are responsible for any customs fees or duties incurred during the return shipment. Refunds for international orders may take an additional 5-10 business days to process due to cross-border banking timelines.

10. LATE OR MISSING REFUNDS
If a refund has not appeared after 10 business days of approval, customers should first check with their bank or credit card company, as processing times vary. If the issue persists, customers should contact support with their order ID for further investigation.

11. SUSPICIOUS OR FRAUDULENT RETURN ACTIVITY
Urban Thread Co. reserves the right to deny refunds or flag accounts that show patterns of return abuse, including but not limited to: excessive return frequency, returning used or altered items, or returns linked to flagged fraudulent orders. Such cases will be escalated for manual review before any refund is issued.

12. HOW TO PLACE AN ORDER
Browse the catalog, click Add to Cart, go to Cart page, fill in your name/email/country and click Place Order. Each order gets a unique Order ID (e.g. O2001).

13. SHIPPING INFORMATION
We ship to Pakistan, USA, Germany, and most international destinations. Domestic: 5-10 business days. International: 10-20 business days. Free shipping on orders above $100 within Pakistan.

14. PAYMENT METHODS
We accept Credit Cards, Debit Cards, and PayPal. All transactions are securely processed.

15. ORDER CANCELLATION
Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placement if not yet dispatched. Contact support with your Order ID. Once dispatched, the return process applies.
